How to play

How to get money:

You get money each time you left click on the pizza, and the amount you receive is displayed beside your cursor. You can also see how much you get per click/second, which is shown above the pizza.

How upgrades/workers work:

In the right side there is a menu displaying different toppings and at the bottom worker/worker speed. The toppings gives you more money per click, and the more expensive toppings gives you more money per click. Each time you buy a upgrade it gets increasingly more expensive. Each worker you buy clicks once a second for you, but you can increase their speed by buying worker speed, which gives all workers the speed increase.

Boost Pizza:

If you are lucky a boost pizza will appear on your screen, which gives you a 10x boost to both clicks & dollars pr. click. The boost only lasts 10 seconds so take advantage of those few seconds to click away! The boost appears within 10-100 seconds after a boost pizza.

Good to know:

The game saves automatically every minute, which is announced when a box pops up at the bottom of your screen. The game also saves each time you buy an upgrade.
