How to play

How to get money:

You earn money every time you left-click on the pizza. The amount you earn is displayed next to your cursor. You can also see how much money you earn per click and per second, which is displayed above the pizza.

How upgrades/workers work:

On the right side of the screen, you'll find a menu displaying different toppings, as well as workers and worker speed at the bottom.

The toppings increase the amount of money you earn per click. More expensive toppings provide a greater increase in money per click. However, each time you purchase an upgrade, its price increases.

Each worker automatically clicks the pizza once per second, earning money for you. You can increase their speed by purchasing worker speed upgrades, which increase the speed of all your workers.

Boost Pizza:

If you're lucky, a Boost Pizza will appear on your screen, giving you a 10x boost to both your clicks and the money you earn per click.

The boost only lasts for 10 seconds, so make the most of those few seconds and click away! A new Boost Pizza can appear anywhere between 10 and 100 seconds after the previous one.

Good to know:

The game automatically saves your progress every minute. You'll be notified when this happens through a pop-up box at the bottom of your screen.

Your progress is also saved every time you purchase an upgrade.
